\*\*Statistical Challenges in Uncertainty Quantification for Supervised Learning\*\*



Rafael Izbicki (UFSCar)



\*\*Abstract:\*\* Uncertainty quantification is becoming increasingly important in

supervised learning. In this talk, I will discuss several statistical

challenges that arise when we aim not only to predict accurately, but

also to evaluate predictive uncertainty.



I will focus on three problems: conditional density estimation,

goodness-of-fit for predictive distributions, and prediction sets with

finite-sample guarantees. I will also discuss recent developments,

including tabular foundation models and conformal prediction, with

examples from scientific applications.
